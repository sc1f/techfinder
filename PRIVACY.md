# TechFinder Privacy Policy

TechFinder collects no personal data.

- **Camera.** TechFinder uses the iPhone camera only to show a live viewfinder and to meter light. It doesn't record, save or send images or video. Nothing from the camera leaves your iPhone.
- **Your settings.** Your lenses, formats, exposure limits and other settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** TechFinder doesn't use analytics or advertising services, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and makes no network connections.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/sc1f/techfinder/issues>.
